# Representative recipe content, 2026-09-13

Fridge inventory is Reffi's core service. Recipes help users decide how to use that stock. The catalog contains 309 recipes in `Reffi/Resources/recipes-seed.json`.

Eighteen recipes contain bilingual amounts for every ingredient, using their listed servings: kimchi stew, doenjang stew, kimchi fried rice, egg fried rice, rolled omelette, bean sprout soup, braised tofu, stir-fried bok choy, tomato and egg stir-fry, tomato pasta, aglio e olio, scrambled eggs, French toast, grilled cheese, miso soup, caprese salad, pesto pasta and cucumber raita. The other 291 recipes remain compatible without amounts.

Miso soup now specifies the listed 2 g of dried wakame during preparation. Raita timing includes its chilling step. These are authored starting recipes; they have not been validated through a cooking trial.

The kitchen-copy sheet shows amounts before the steps. Amounts are cooking guidance, not an inventory sufficiency check or automatic consumption calculation. Every stock ingredient must now be reviewed at completion: enter the remaining quantity or explicitly choose Used it all. Remaining quantities start blank; no arbitrary half portion is assumed.

Existing safety instructions were compared with [USDA food safety basics](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/steps-keep-food-safe) and [USDA egg guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/eggs/shell-eggs-farm-table). Reheated rice dishes specify 74°C / 165°F and amended egg dishes specify 71°C / 160°F. Timing is an estimate and does not replace the stated doneness check.

YouTube remains an external search chosen by the user, in the current app language. Website copy uses 250+ and over 250 recipes as a stable lower-bound claim.
